<objective>
Compose the per-turn JSONL row: the pure function that turns a finalized
`AssistantMessage` plus turn timing into a `TurnRow`.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 3 — "every turn appends a JSONL row carrying
model, provider, tokens, cost, latency, cache-read ratio and outcome — and no
reasoning-token column for Bedrock, which reports none". Two rules are easy to break
silently: `reasoning: 0` on a Bedrock row is a fabricated measurement (locked
decision 6), and a cost assertion written naively against `fauxProvider` is
`0 === 0` because faux hardcodes `usage.cost` to zeros.

Output: `turns.ts` and its inline-fixture unit test. The hook wiring and the
end-to-end session proof are plan 02-08.
</objective>
