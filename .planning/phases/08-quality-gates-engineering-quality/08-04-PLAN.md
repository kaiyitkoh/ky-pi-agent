<objective>
Build QUAL-09's ledger half: the wall-clock and token cost the quality layer adds becomes a RECORDED NUMBER PER
TURN in the JSONL, so the correctness/latency balance is measured and tunable rather than guessed.

This plan lands the accumulator and the field so the producers (plan 08-06's deterministic gate stage, plan 08-07's
reviewer, plan 08-09's refute pass) can call `addQualityCost(...)` in their own plans without a second edit to
`diagnostics/`. Layering is deliberate: the accumulator lives in `diagnostics/` because diagnostics owns the ledger;
`quality/` is a producer that writes into it. Diagnostics never imports `quality/`.

`TurnRow.latencyMs` already exists as the TURN's wall clock. The new fields are the quality layer's SHARE of it —
state that distinction in code and in the doc, or the field is meaningless.

The omission rule is binding: an optional field is genuinely ABSENT from the JSON, never present-and-`null` and
never present-and-`0`. A ratio of nothing is not zero.

Purpose: criterion 5 of the phase's success criteria.
Output: accumulator + TurnRow fields + doc + unit and ledger-level tests.
</objective>
