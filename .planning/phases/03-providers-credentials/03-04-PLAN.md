<objective>
Assert what Pi *composes* from the template, not what the template *says*.

Purpose: typebox's `Type.Object` permits additional properties, so a typo'd compat key —
`supportsStoore: false` — validates silently and does nothing (Trap T6, EXECUTED in
RESEARCH §Q2.1). That is the single most likely way this phase ships a broken provider. And
because the DashScope model ids are deliberately identical to the built-in DeepSeek-direct
ids, copying `contextWindow: 1000000` across is one keystroke away and invisible in review
(Trap T9) — PROV-06's invariant is what makes it visible.

Output: `tests/unit/dashscope-compat.test.ts` and `tests/unit/model-metadata.test.ts`.
</objective>
