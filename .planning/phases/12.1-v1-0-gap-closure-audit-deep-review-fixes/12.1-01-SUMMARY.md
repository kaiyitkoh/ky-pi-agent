## Facts Plan 02 must publish in docs/SAFETY.md

- Subagent guard: on `session_start` the safety entry file is registered as required child extension `ky-safety` with `requireForAllRunners: true`, so every child loads it regardless of the agent's `extensions`; external-CLI runners are rejected by pi-subagents. A `subagent` launch is blocked unless registration succeeded for the current session id; `session_shutdown` disposes it.
- Management: read-only actions are an allow-list (guide, list, get, models, status, children.list, validate, doctor, inspector.status, project.status, steer, resume, stop); everything else, including unknown or non-string actions, needs `ctx.ui.confirm` and is refused with no UI.
- Confirm rule: allowed in `tui` and `rpc` when `hasUI`; json, print and subagent children refuse. Timeout 300000 ms, a timeout or cancel refuses.
- Agent definitions and settings: writes to `<project>/.pi/agents/**`, `<project>/.agents/**`, `~/.pi/agent/agents/**`, `~/.pi/agent/settings.json`, `<project>/.pi/settings.json` are blocked.
- Reads blocked now: any case of `.env*` (examples/templates still allowed), NTFS `name:stream` suffixes, 8.3 short-name aliases (fail closed, rename to the long name), all of `~/.aws/**` and `~/.ssh/**`, `~/.git-credentials`, `~/.docker/config.json`, `~/.pi/agent/auth.json`. EV-56 and EV-57 are now caught (move to BLOCKED).

Note a bare `*` basename glob matches `.env` and therefore blocks; Plan 02 decides how policy.ts applies it.
