## D-01 wording (for docs)

A task passes when it produced a commit AND the plan's declared verification command passes at the end. Intermediate non-zero exits, failed tool calls (no-match grep, red-before-fix test, edit with an unmatched old string) and blocked-then-approved calls are logged, not fatal.
